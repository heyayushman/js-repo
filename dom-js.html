Doraemon is a Japanese manga series created by Fujiko F. Fujio, the pen name of manga writing duo Hiroshi Fujimoto and Motoo Abiko. The series follows the adventures of a robotic cat named Doraemon, who travels back in time from the 22nd century to help a young boy named Nobita Nobi navigate the challenges of everyday life. Doraemon possesses a four-dimensional pocket from which he can produce various futuristic gadgets to assist Nobita in his endeavors.
